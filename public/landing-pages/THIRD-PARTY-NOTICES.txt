6. Fonts not redistributed here
==============================================================================

KageLandingPage can optionally switch its typography to Instrument Serif,
Newsreader or Geist. Those fonts are not included in these files; the component
requests them from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) only when one is selected.
The /experiments/kage route uses the page's default typography (Onest), so no
external font is requested.
